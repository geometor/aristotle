Ὄργανον (Organon Geometricum) — Bekker Greek (1831) & Oxford English Translation

ΑΡΙΣΤΟΤΕΛΟΥΣ ΟΡΓΑΝΟΝ
ORGANON GEOMETRICUM

Author: Aristotle of Stagira (384–322 BC)
Critical Greek Text: Immanuel Bekker (Berlin: Prussian Academy, 1831)
English Translation: E. M. Edghill, A. J. Jenkinson, W. A. Pickard-Cambridge (Oxford Clarendon Press)
GEOMETOR Digital Edition: Synchronized English, Greek (Bekker), Summary & Notes Tracks

«Τούτου δὲ τὸ μὲν διωρισμένον ἐστί, τὸ δὲ συνεχές.»

“Quantity is either discrete or continuous.” — Categoriae 6, 4b20

Table of Contents

Categories — On Quantity (Categoriae 6)

Foundational taxonomy of discrete quantity (number, speech) vs. continuous quantity (line, surface, solid body, time, and place).

Prior Analytics — Syllogistic Deduction & Incommensurability

Four chapters on first-figure universal syllogism, the reduction per impossibile of the incommensurability of the diagonal, and circularity in parallel-line proofs.

Topics — Definitions & Proportion via Antanairesis

Intelligible priority in geometric definitions and the pre-Eudoxian definition of proportion through reciprocal subtraction (antanairesis).
