.. py:class:: UnitCommentary

   Editorial synopsis and analytical commentary for one Book Codex unit.

   :ivar thesis: Concise architectural thesis of the unit.
   :ivar argument_steps: Ordered structural breakdown of the chapters in the unit.
   :ivar heath_analysis: Historical-mathematical commentary engaging Sir Thomas Heath
                         and Euclid's *Elements*.
   :ivar hoenen_analysis: Epistemological-noetic commentary engaging Fr. Peter Hoenen S.J.,
                          St. Thomas Aquinas, and Fra Luca Pacioli.
   :ivar crux_notes: Detailed locus-by-locus philological and mathematical notes.
