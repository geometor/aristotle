===================================
Topics VIII.3 — Structural Synopsis
===================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 158a31–159a14

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Topics* VI.4 (`141a23–142b19`) and VIII.3 (`158a31–159a14`), :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` establishes the canon of scientific :term:`definition <horismos>`—insisting that geometric entities must be defined through what is *prior and more intelligible by nature* rather than through sensible posterior effects—and preserves the priceless pre-Eudoxian definition of geometric proportion through :term:`antanairesis <antanairesis>` (reciprocal subtraction, `158b29–35`).

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Topics VIII.3 (158a31–159a14) — Primary Hypotheses & Proportion via Antanairesis (158b29–35)**

Explains why certain arguments are difficult to attack or establish because they stand near primary hypotheses, illustrating how in mathematics even proving that Parallelograms/Triangles of the same height are proportional to their bases (:cite:`Elements VI.1 <euclid:elements>`) was once arduous before the clear definition of 'having the same ratio' via *antanairesis* (`τὴν αὐτὴν ἀνταναίρεσιν ἔχει τὰ χωρία καὶ αἱ βάσεις`, `158b33–34`).
