=================================
Topics VI.4 — Structural Synopsis
=================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 141a23–142b19

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Topics* VI.4 (`141a23–142b19`) and VIII.3 (`158a31–159a14`), :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` establishes the canon of scientific :term:`definition <horismos>`—insisting that geometric entities must be defined through what is *prior and more intelligible by nature* rather than through sensible posterior effects—and preserves the priceless pre-Eudoxian definition of geometric proportion through :term:`antanairesis <antanairesis>` (reciprocal subtraction, `158b29–35`).

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Topics VI.4 (141a23–142b19) — Intelligible Priority in Defining Point, Line, Plane & Solid**

Distinguishes what is better known 'to us' (*hēmin*, sensible solid bodies) from what is better known 'unqualifiedly / by nature' (*haplōs*, point -> line -> plane -> solid). Critiques definitions that invert natural priority by defining point as 'limit of a line' or line as 'limit of a plane' (`141b19–22`), as well as circular definitions.
