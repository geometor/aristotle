==============================================
Posterior Analytics I.27 — Structural Synopsis
==============================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 87a31–87a37

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Analytica Posteriora* I.24, I.27, I.31, and II.19 (`85a–88a`, `99b–100b`), :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` completes the epistemology of demonstrative science by establishing why commensurate universal proof excels particular proof, why arithmetic exceeds geometry in :term:`exactitude <akribeia>`, why sensory observation (*aisthēsis*) can never cross the threshold into scientific necessity (`87b35`), and how :term:`induction <epagoge>` from sense-perception culminates in :term:`nous <nous>` as the principle of principles.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**I.27 (87a31–87a37) — Exactitude (Akribeia): Monad without Position vs. Point with Position**

Ranks theoretical sciences by conceptual simplicity: a science from fewer principles is prior to and more exact than one 'by addition' (*ek prostheseōs*)—as arithmetic (`monas` = substance without position) is prior to geometry (`stigmē` = substance having position).
