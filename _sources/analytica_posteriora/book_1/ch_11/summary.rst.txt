==============================================
Posterior Analytics I.11 — Structural Synopsis
==============================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 77a5–77a35

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Analytica Posteriora* I.1–13 (`71a1–79a16`), :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` constructs the first rigorous meta-theory of deductive mathematical science (:term:`epistēmē <episteme>`). Answering :person:`Plato's <plato>` *Meno* paradox (`71a29`; :cite:`Meno 80d <plato:meno>`), Aristotle demonstrates that all intellectual teaching proceeds from pre-existent knowledge of two irreducible kinds—comprehension of meaning (*ti sēmainei*) and grasp of existence (*hoti estin*)—and classifies the indemonstrable starting points of geometry into :term:`definitions <horismos>`, :term:`hypotheses <hypothesis>`, :term:`postulates <aitema>`, and :term:`common axioms <axioma>`.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**I.10–11 (76a31–77a35) — The Architecture of Mathematical First Principles**

Draws the canonical distinction between common :term:`axioms <axioma>` ('equals subtracted from equals leave equals'), existential :term:`hypotheses <hypothesis>` (the unit, points, and lines), :term:`definitions <horismos>` (which state essence without asserting existence), and constructive :term:`postulates <aitema>` (`76b23–34`). Defends geometry against Platonic separate Forms (`77a5`).
