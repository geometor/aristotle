:title: GEOMETOR • aristotle

====================
GEOMETOR • aristotle
====================

.. rubric:: *Loci Geometrici et Noetici* — Aristotle on Demonstrative Science, Quantity, Abstraction & the Continuum

Critical multi-track Book Codex gathering the **fifty-four foundational chapters** across eight treatises of :person:`Aristotle of Stagira <aristotle>` (384–322 BC) cited throughout the GEOMETOR historical canon—from :person:`Sir Thomas Heath's <heath>` commentary on :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` *Elements* (``geometor/euclid``) and :person:`Fra Luca Pacioli's <pacioli>` *De Divina Proportione* (``geometor/pacioli``) to :person:`Fr. Peter Hoenen S.J.'s <hoenen>` *De Noetica Geometriae* (``geometor/hoenen``).

Every treatise unit is presented across **four synchronized tracks** powered by ``photon-platform/publish``:

1. **English** — Verbatim public-domain Clarendon Press *Oxford Translation* (:person:`Edghill <edghill>`, :person:`Jenkinson <jenkinson>`, :person:`Mure <mure>`, :person:`Pickard-Cambridge <pickard-cambridge>`, :person:`Hardie <hardie>` & :person:`Gaye <gaye>`, :person:`Stocks <stocks>`, :person:`J. A. Smith <smith-ja>`, and :person:`W. D. Ross <ross>`) anchored to exact Bekker columns.
2. **Greek (Bekker)** — Verbatim polytonic Greek from :person:`Immanuel Bekker's <bekker>` Prussian Academy edition (*Aristotelis Opera*, 1831/1837) and :person:`W. D. Ross's <ross>` Oxford critical edition of the *Metaphysics* (1924).
3. **Summary** — Argumentative architecture, chapter-by-chapter Bekker spans, and cross-references across Libri I–V.
4. **Critical Notes** — Dual-tradition commentary uniting **Heath's** reconstruction of pre-Euclidean Greek mathematics with **Hoenen's** and :person:`St. Thomas Aquinas's <thomas-aquinas>` scholastic noetics of intellectual insight in the :term:`phantasm <phantasma>` and :term:`intelligible matter <hyle-noete>`.
