==================================
Physics IV.1 — Structural Synopsis
==================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 208a27–209a30

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber III.B unites the crowning physical-geometrical analyses of spatial order, continuity, and dimensionality: *Physics* IV.1–5 (`208a27–213a11`) defining :term:`place <topos>` as the innermost motionless boundary of the containing body, *Physics* VI.1–2 & VI.9 (`231a21–233b32`, `239b5–240a18`) proving that no :term:`continuum <syneches>` can be composed of indivisible points and resolving :person:`Zeno's <zeno>` four paradoxes, and *De Caelo* I.1 (`268a1–b10`) demonstrating the completeness of three spatial dimensions.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Physics IV.1–3 (208a27–210b31) — Existence & Aporiai of Place (Topos)**

Establishes the reality of place from mutual replacement (*antimetastasis*, `208b1`) and natural directional motions, notes that mathematical objects, though lacking physical place, exhibit positional right/left relative to us (`208b22–25`), refutes :person:`Plato's <plato>` identification of space (*chōra*) with matter in the :cite:`Timaeus <plato:timaeus>` (`209b11–17`), and distinguishes eight senses of 'in' (`210a14–24`).
