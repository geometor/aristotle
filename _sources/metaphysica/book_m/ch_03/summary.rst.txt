Metaphysics M (XIII).3 — Structural Synopsis
============================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 1077b17–1078b6

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber V.B crowns the entire *Loci Geometrici et Noetici* Codex with the three supreme peaks of Aristotelian philosophy of mathematics: *Metaphysics* Z.10–11 (`1034b20–1037a20`) introducing **intelligible matter** (:term:`hylē noētē <hyle-noete>`, `1036a9–12`), *Metaphysics* $\Theta$.9 (`1051a4–33`) revealing that geometrical theorems are discovered by **actualizing** (:term:`energeia <energeia>`, `1051a21–33`) latent divisions in construction, and *Metaphysics* M.1–3 (`1076a8–1078b6`) articulating the *qua*-ontology of mathematicals and celebrating **mathematical beauty** (:term:`to kalon <kalon>`: order, symmetry, and definiteness, `1078a31–b6`).

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Metaphysics M (XIII).3 (1077b17–1078b6) — Qua-Predication & Mathematical Beauty (1078a31–b6)**

Establishes Aristotle's positive **qua-theory** (`ᾗ`): just as truths hold of moving things *qua* moving apart from what they are, geometry studies sensible bodies *not qua sensible, but qua lengths and planes* (`1077b22–34`), achieving higher :term:`exactitude <akribeia>` the simpler its aspect (`1078a9–17`). Closes with the sublime defence of **mathematical beauty** (`1078a31–b6`): whereas the good concerns action, *the beautiful (`τὸ καλόν`) exists also in motionless things*, and its supreme forms are **order (`τάξις`), symmetry (`συμμετρία`), and definiteness (`τὸ ὡρισμένον`)**, which the mathematical sciences pre-eminently demonstrate!
