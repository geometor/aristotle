===========================================
Metaphysics B (III).2 — Structural Synopsis
===========================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 996a18–998a19

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber V.A gathers the architectonic chapters of :person:`Aristotle's <aristotle>` *Metaphysics* on axioms, mathematical objects, unity, quantity, and the classification of the sciences: Book B.2 (`996a18–998a19`) raising the sharp aporiai of sensible vs. ideal geometry (:person:`Protagoras's <protagoras>` circle-tangent objection, `998a2–4`), Book $\Delta$.6 & 13 (`1015b16–1017a6`, `1020a7–32`) defining the indivisible measure and :term:`quantity <poson>`, and Book E.1 (`1025b3–1026a32`) establishing the canonical tripartition of theoretical philosophy into physics, mathematics, and theology/first philosophy.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Metaphysics B (III).2 (996a18–998a19) — Aporiai of Axioms & Mathematical Intermediates**

Asks whether one science studies both substance and the common :term:`axioms <axioma>` used by all mathematicians (`996b26–33`), and confronts the dilemma of :person:`Plato's <plato>` 'intermediates' (*ta metaxy*): sensible lines are not straight as the geometer defines them, nor does a physical circle touch a ruler at a single point as :person:`Protagoras <protagoras>` objected (`997b35–998a6`), yet positing a separate duplicate heaven and duplicate mathematical solids generates absurdities (`997b12–34`).
