============================================
Metaphysics Z (VII).10 — Structural Synopsis
============================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 1034b20–1036a25

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber V.B crowns the entire *Loci Geometrici et Noetici* Codex with the three supreme peaks of Aristotelian philosophy of mathematics: *Metaphysics* Z.10–11 (`1034b20–1037a20`) introducing **intelligible matter** (:term:`hylē noētē <hyle-noete>`, `1036a9–12`), *Metaphysics* $\Theta$.9 (`1051a4–33`) revealing that geometrical theorems are discovered by **actualizing** (:term:`energeia <energeia>`, `1051a21–33`) latent divisions in construction, and *Metaphysics* M.1–3 (`1076a8–1078b6`) articulating the *qua*-ontology of mathematicals and celebrating **mathematical beauty** (:term:`to kalon <kalon>`: order, symmetry, and definiteness, `1078a31–b6`).

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Metaphysics Z (VII).10–11 (1034b20–1037a20) — Formal Parts vs. Intelligible Matter (1036a9)**

Solves why 'semicircle' is *not* part of the definition of 'circle', whereas 'right angle' *is* presupposed in the definition of 'acute angle' (`1035b4–12`): semicircles are parts of an individual circle's **intelligible matter** (`ὕλη δὲ ἡ μὲν αἰσθητή ἐστιν ἡ δὲ νοητή, αἰσθητὴ μὲν οἷον χαλκὸς καὶ ξύλον... νοητὴ δὲ ἡ ἐν τοῖς αἰσθητοῖς ὑπάρχουσα μὴ ᾗ αἰσθητά, οἷον τὰ μαθηματικά`, `1036a9–12`). Even when all bronze and stone are abstracted, an individual mathematical circle still possesses pure spatial extension individuating its congruent parts (`1036b32–1037a5`).
