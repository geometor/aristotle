=========================================
Metaphysics Δ (V).6 — Structural Synopsis
=========================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 1015b16–1017a6

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber V.A gathers the architectonic chapters of :person:`Aristotle's <aristotle>` *Metaphysics* on axioms, mathematical objects, unity, quantity, and the classification of the sciences: Book B.2 (`996a18–998a19`) raising the sharp aporiai of sensible vs. ideal geometry (:person:`Protagoras's <protagoras>` circle-tangent objection, `998a2–4`), Book $\Delta$.6 & 13 (`1015b16–1017a6`, `1020a7–32`) defining the indivisible measure and :term:`quantity <poson>`, and Book E.1 (`1025b3–1026a32`) establishing the canonical tripartition of theoretical philosophy into physics, mathematics, and theology/first philosophy.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Metaphysics Δ (V).6 (1015b16–1017a6) — Ontological Lexicon of Unity & the Indivisible Measure**

Defines 'the one' (*to hen*) by continuity (`1015b36`), wholeness of form, and above all **being the primary indivisible measure of each genus** (`τὸ γὰρ ἓν εἶναι ἀρχή τινί ἐστιν ἀριθμοῦ εἶναι· τὸ γὰρ πρῶτον μέτρον ἀρχή`, `1016b17–21`). Distinguishes indivisible in every respect and without position (:term:`monad <monas>`) from indivisible in every respect with position (:term:`point <stigme>`, `1016b24–26`), one-dimensionally divisible (line), two-dimensionally (plane), and three-dimensionally (body).
