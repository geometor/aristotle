====================================================
Book E (VI) — Tripartition of Theoretical Philosophy
====================================================

Classification of theoretical sciences into physics (separable but unmovable? no, inseparable and mobile), mathematics (inseparable but immobile), and theology/first philosophy.

.. epigraph::

   *«ὥστε τρεῖς ἂν εἶεν φιλοσοφίαι θεωρητικαί, μαθηματική, φυσική, θεολογική.»*

   “There must, then, be three theoretical philosophies, mathematics, physics, and what we may call theology.” — **Metaphysica E.1, 1026a18–19**
