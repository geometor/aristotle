=======================
Bibliography & Editions
=======================

.. rubric:: Critical Greek Editions, Oxford Translations, Classical Treatises & Commentaries

Authoritative editions, translations, classical mathematical propositions, and commentaries cited across the primary apparatus and analytical notes of *Loci Geometrici et Noetici*.

.. _cite-bekker-1831:

**Bekker, Immanuel (ed.) — Aristotelis Opera, edidit Academia Regia Borussica, Vols. I–II (Berlin: Georg Reimer, 1831; Oxford repr. 1837)**

- **Citation Key**: ``bekker:1831``  |  **Locus**: ``1a–1462b``
- **Significance**: Canonical Greek critical text defining universal Bekker page, column (a/b), and line coordinates.

.. _cite-ross-metaphysics-1924:

**Ross, Sir William David (ed.) — Aristotle's Metaphysics: A Revised Text with Introduction and Commentary, 2 vols. (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1924)**

- **Citation Key**: ``ross:metaphysics:1924``  |  **Locus**: ``980a–1093b``
- **Significance**: Standard critical Greek edition and commentary for Liber V of this Codex.

.. _cite-ross-physics-1936:

**Ross, Sir William David (ed.) — Aristotle's Physics: A Revised Text with Introduction and Commentary (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1936)**

- **Citation Key**: ``ross:physics:1936``  |  **Locus**: ``184a–267b``
- **Significance**: Standard critical commentary on Aristotle's abstraction, infinite, place, and continuum treatises.

.. _cite-ross-analytics-1949:

**Ross, Sir William David (ed.) — Aristotle's Prior and Posterior Analytics: A Revised Text with Introduction and Commentary (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1949)**

- **Citation Key**: ``ross:analytics:1949``  |  **Locus**: ``24a–100b``
- **Significance**: Comprehensive philological and logical commentary on the Analytics.

.. _cite-waitz-organon-1844:

**Waitz, Theodor (ed.) — Aristotelis Organon Graece, 2 vols. (Leipzig: Hahn, 1844–1846)**

- **Citation Key**: ``waitz:organon:1844``  |  **Locus**: ``Vols. I–II``
- **Significance**: Nineteenth-century critical edition and Latin commentary cited throughout Heath and Hoenen.

.. _cite-bonitz-index-1870:

**Bonitz, Hermann — Index Aristotelicus, Aristotelis Opera Vol. V (Berlin: Georg Reimer, 1870)**

- **Citation Key**: ``bonitz:index:1870``  |  **Locus**: ``pp. 1–878``
- **Significance**: Exhaustive lexicon of Aristotle's Greek philosophical and mathematical terminology.

.. _cite-oxford-works-1908:

**Smith, J. A., and W. D. Ross (eds.) — The Works of Aristotle Translated into English, Vols. I–III, VIII (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1908–1931)**

- **Citation Key**: ``oxford:works:1908``  |  **Locus**: ``Vols. I, II, III, VIII``
- **Significance**: Public-domain Oxford English translations by Edghill, Jenkinson, Mure, Pickard-Cambridge, Hardie & Gaye, Stocks, Smith, and Ross staged in this Codex.

.. _cite-heath-euclid-1926:

**Heath, Sir Thomas Little — The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements, 2nd ed., 3 vols. (Cambridge University Press, 1926) [geometor/euclid]**

- **Citation Key**: ``heath:euclid:1926``  |  **Locus**: ``Vols. I–III``
- **Significance**: Contains 145 distinct Aristotelian locus citations reconstructing pre-Euclidean definitions, postulates, and proofs.

.. _cite-heath-math-aristotle-1949:

**Heath, Sir Thomas Little — Mathematics in Aristotle (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1949)**

- **Citation Key**: ``heath:math-aristotle:1949``  |  **Locus**: ``pp. 1–291``
- **Significance**: Systematic treatise-by-treatise mathematical commentary on every geometrical and arithmetical passage in the Aristotelian corpus.

.. _cite-hoenen-noetica-1952:

**Hoenen, Petrus Hubertus Jacobus, S.J. — De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis, Analecta Gregoriana Vol. 56 (Rome: Pontificia Universitas Gregoriana, 1952) [geometor/hoenen]**

- **Citation Key**: ``hoenen:noetica:1952``  |  **Locus**: ``Cap. I–VII & Appendix``
- **Significance**: Contains 64 Aristotelian locus keys developing the noetics of intellectual intuition in the phantasm and intelligible matter.

.. _cite-pacioli-divina-1509:

**Pacioli, Fra Luca, O.F.M. — De Divina Proportione (Venice: Paganino Paganini, 1509) [geometor/pacioli]**

- **Citation Key**: ``pacioli:divina:1509``  |  **Locus**: ``Compendio Cap. I–V & Architectura``
- **Significance**: Renaissance treatise citing Aristotle on mathematical certitude, subalternation of perspective/music, and the five regular solids.

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

**Euclid of Alexandria — Elements (Στοιχεῖα, ed. J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig, 1883–1888)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:elements``  |  **Locus**: ``Books I–XIII``
- **Significance**: The canonical Greek synthesis of definitions, postulates, common notions, plane geometry, Eudoxian proportion, irrationals, and stereometry.

.. _cite-euclid-i-5:

**Euclid of Alexandria — Elements, Book I, Proposition 5 (Pons Asinorum)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:I.5``  |  **Locus**: ``I.5``
- **Significance**: Equality of base angles in an isosceles triangle; contrasted with Aristotle's older mixed-angle proof in Prior Analytics I.24 (41b13–22).

.. _cite-euclid-i-29:

**Euclid of Alexandria — Elements, Book I, Postulate 5 & Proposition 29 (Theory of Parallels)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:I.29``  |  **Locus**: ``Post. 5; I.27–29``
- **Significance**: Euclid's resolution of the circularity in parallel-line proofs diagnosed by Aristotle in Prior Analytics II.16 (65a4–9).

.. _cite-euclid-i-32:

**Euclid of Alexandria — Elements, Book I, Proposition 32 (Interior Angles of a Triangle = 2R)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:I.32``  |  **Locus**: ``I.32``
- **Significance**: Proved via the parallel through the vertex; cited in Posterior Analytics I.5 (74a25–32), I.31 (87b35), and Metaphysics Θ.9 (1051a24–26).

.. _cite-euclid-iii-31:

**Euclid of Alexandria — Elements, Book III, Proposition 31 (Angle in a Semicircle is Right)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:III.31``  |  **Locus**: ``III.31``
- **Significance**: Proved in Metaphysics Θ.9 (1051a26–29) and Posterior Analytics II.11 (94a28–34) by erecting the radius from the center of the diameter.

.. _cite-euclid-v-def-5:

**Euclid of Alexandria / Eudoxus of Cnidus — Elements, Book V, Definition 5 & Proposition 16 (General Proportion & Alternation)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:V.def.5``  |  **Locus**: ``V.Def.5; V.16``
- **Significance**: General Eudoxian theory of proportion heralded in Posterior Analytics I.5 (74a17–25) as superseding separate proofs for numbers, lines, solids, and times.

.. _cite-euclid-x-2:

**Euclid of Alexandria / Theaetetus — Elements, Book X, Propositions 2–3 (Anthyphairetic Criterion of Incommensurability)**

- **Citation Key**: ``euclid:X.2``  |  **Locus**: ``X.2–3``
- **Significance**: Geometric preservation of reciprocal subtraction (antanairesis), attested in Topics VIII.3 (158b29–35).

.. _cite-plato-meno:

**Plato — Meno**

- **Citation Key**: ``plato:meno``  |  **Locus**: ``80d–86c``
- **Significance**: Meno's paradox of inquiry and the geometric doubling of the square, explicitly answered in Posterior Analytics I.1 (71a29).

.. _cite-plato-republic:

**Plato — Republic, Books VI–VII (Divided Line & Quadrivium)**

- **Citation Key**: ``plato:republic``  |  **Locus**: ``509d–511e, 525a–534d``
- **Significance**: Distinction between discursive mathematical dianoia from hypotheses and dialectical noēsis.

.. _cite-plato-timaeus:

**Plato — Timaeus**

- **Citation Key**: ``plato:timaeus``  |  **Locus**: ``48e–56c``
- **Significance**: Identification of receptacle space (chōra) with matter and construction of physical bodies out of elementary plane triangles—critiqued in Physics IV.2 and De Caelo III.7–8.

.. _cite-proclus-in-euclidem:

**Proclus Diadochus — In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii (ed. G. Friedlein, Leipzig, 1873)**

- **Citation Key**: ``proclus:in-euclidem``  |  **Locus**: ``pp. 48–84, 178–207``
- **Significance**: Preserves the Eudemian summary of Greek geometry and compares Aristotelian axioms, postulates, and hypotheses with Euclid's Elements.

.. _cite-alexander-in-metaph:

**Alexander of Aphrodisias — In Aristotelis Metaphysica Commentaria (ed. M. Hayduck, CAG I, Berlin, 1891)**

- **Citation Key**: ``alexander:in-metaph``  |  **Locus**: ``CAG I``
- **Significance**: Classical Peripatetic commentary on Metaphysics A–Δ (with pseudo-Alexander / Michael of Ephesus on E–N).

.. _cite-simplicius-in-physica:

**Simplicius of Cilicia — In Aristotelis Physicorum Libros Commentaria (ed. H. Diels, CAG IX–X, Berlin, 1882–1895)**

- **Citation Key**: ``simplicius:in-physica``  |  **Locus**: ``CAG IX–X``
- **Significance**: Essential source on Antiphon, Bryson, Hippocrates of Chios, and Zeno's continuum arguments.

.. _cite-philoponus-in-anal-post:

**John Philoponus — In Aristotelis Analytica Priora et Posteriora Commentaria (ed. M. Wallies, CAG XIII.2–3, Berlin, 1905–1909)**

- **Citation Key**: ``philoponus:in-anal-post``  |  **Locus**: ``CAG XIII.2–3``
- **Significance**: Preserves ancient reconstructions of Aristotle's geometric examples in the Analytics.

.. _cite-aquinas-in-anal-post:

**Thomas Aquinas, St., O.P. — Expositio Libri Posteriorum Analyticorum (Leonine ed., Vol. I*2, Rome, 1989)**

- **Citation Key**: ``aquinas:in-anal-post``  |  **Locus**: ``Lib. I–II``
- **Significance**: Scholastic exposition of demonstrative science, axioms vs. postulates, subalternation, and intuitive epagōgē.

.. _cite-aquinas-in-boeth-trin:

**Thomas Aquinas, St., O.P. — Super Boethium De Trinitate (Leonine ed., Vol. L, Rome, 1992)**

- **Citation Key**: ``aquinas:in-boeth-trin``  |  **Locus**: ``qq. 5–6``
- **Significance**: Locus classicus on the three degrees of theoretical distinction/abstraction and the termination of mathematical judgment in the imagination (ad imaginationem).

.. _cite-averroes-in-metaph:

**Averroes (Ibn Rushd) — Aristotelis Metaphysicorum Libri XIIII cum Averrois Cordubensis in eosdem Commentariis (Venice: Junta, 1562)**

- **Citation Key**: ``averroes:in-metaph``  |  **Locus**: ``Lib. II, comm. 14–16``
- **Significance**: Classical Peripatetic-Arabic exposition of Metaphysics α.3 (995a14–17) on mathematical exactitude (akribeia) and abstraction from sensible matter.

.. _cite-hilbert-grundlagen-1899:

**Hilbert, David — Grundlagen der Geometrie (Leipzig: Teubner, 1899)**

- **Citation Key**: ``hilbert:grundlagen:1899``  |  **Locus**: ``Chs. I–II``
- **Significance**: Modern formalist axiomatization contrasted with Aristotelian intuitive first principles in the Codex commentary.
