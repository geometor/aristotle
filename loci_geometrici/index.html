Loci Geometrici et Noetici — Aristotle on Geometry, Episteme & Nous¶

Synchronized Multi-Track Book Codex (English · Greek Bekker · Summary · Critical Notes)

Loci Geometrici et Noetici gathers into a single critical edition the fifty-four foundational chapters across eight treatises of Aristotle (Categories, Prior Analytics, Posterior Analytics, Topics, Physics, De Caelo, De Anima, and Metaphysics) that govern the deductive architecture of Euclidean geometry, continuous vs. discrete quantity, potential infinity, imaginative construction (phantasma), and formal abstraction in intelligible matter.

Every unit is presented across four synchronized tracks—English (public-domain Oxford Clarendon translations), Greek (Bekker) (verbatim Prussian Academy 1831/1837 & Ross 1924 polytonic Greek), Summary, and Critical Notes synthesizing Sir Thomas Heath's mathematical-historical reconstructions with Fr. Peter Hoenen S.J.'s and St. Thomas Aquinas's scholastic-noetic analysis—indexed to 199 Bekker loci across geometor/euclid, geometor/hoenen, and geometor/pacioli.
