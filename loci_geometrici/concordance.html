Bekker Concordance Across GEOMETOR¶

Master Cross-Corpus Register Linking Euclid, Hoenen, and Pacioli to Bekker (1831)

This concordance indexes 199 distinct Aristotelian Bekker loci (representing 246 textual citation occurrences) mined directly from the three sister historical codices of the GEOMETOR canon:

  • geometor/euclid (Heath, Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements): 145 Aristotle citation keys across Volumes I–III.

  • geometor/hoenen (Hoenen, De Noetica Geometriae): 64 Aristotle citation keys across Capita I–VII & Appendix.

  • geometor/pacioli (Pacioli, De Divina Proportione): 17 Aristotle citation & folio entries across the 1509 Compendio and Architectura.

Of the 199 distinct Bekker loci cited across the sister repositories, 84 foundational geometric and noetic loci fall directly within the fifty-four full chapters edited in Libri I–V of Loci Geometrici et Noetici and link directly to their exact Bekker passage in this Codex.
