On the Soul¶

Περὶ ψυχῆς (De Anima) — Bekker Greek (1831) & Oxford English Translation

ΑΡΙΣΤΟΤΕΛΟΥΣ ΠΕΡΙ ΨΥΧΗΣ
DE ANIMA

Author: Aristotle of Stagira (384–322 BC)
Critical Greek Text: Immanuel Bekker (Berlin: Prussian Academy, 1831)
English Translation: J. A. Smith (Oxford Clarendon Press)
GEOMETOR Digital Edition: Synchronized English, Greek (Bekker), Summary & Notes Tracks

«διὸ οὐδέποτε νοεῖ ἄνευ φαντάσματος ἡ ψυχή.»

“That is why the soul never thinks without an image (phantasma).” — De Anima III.7, 431a16–17

Table of Contents

Book III — Imagination, Phantasma & Intellectual Abstraction

Six chapters on imagination (phantasia), phantasms in mathematical reasoning, the active intellect, and thinking undivided magnitudes.
