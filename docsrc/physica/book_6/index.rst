============================================================
Book VI — Indivisibility of the Continuum & Zeno's Paradoxes
============================================================

Three chapters proving that lines cannot consist of points, establishing the isomorphic division of space, time, and motion, and refuting Zeno.

.. epigraph::

   *«ἀδύνατον ἐξ ἀδιαιρέτων εἶναί τι συνεχές, οἷον γραμμὴν ἐκ στιγμῶν, εἴπερ ἡ γραμμὴ μὲν συνεχές, ἡ στιγμὴ δὲ ἀδιαίρετον.»*

   “It is impossible that anything continuous should be composed of indivisibles: for instance, a line cannot be composed of points, the line being continuous and the point indivisible.” — **Physica VI.1, 231a24–26**
