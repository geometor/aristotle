Prior Analytics I.24 — Structural Synopsis
==========================================

.. rubric:: Thematic Synopsis & Argument Progression · Bekker 41b6–41b35

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Liber II.A gathers :person:`Aristotle's <aristotle>` foundational logical-ontological texts on :term:`quantity <poson>` and geometric deduction from the *Organon*: *Categories* 6 (`4b20–6a35`) establishing the irreducible bifurcation between :term:`discrete <diorismenon>` and :term:`continuous <syneches>` quantity, and *Prior Analytics* I.4, I.23–24, and II.16 (`25b`, `40b–41b`, `64b–65a`) exhibiting syllogistic necessity, the *reductio* proof of the :term:`incommensurability of the diagonal <asymmetron>`, the pre-Euclidean proof of *Elements* I.5, and the circularity lurking in pre-Euclidean parallel-line theories.

Logical & Noetic Progression
----------------------------

**Prior Analytics I.24 (41b6–41b35) — Pre-Euclidean Proof of Isosceles Base Angles (41b13–22)**

Illustrates why every demonstration requires at least one universal premiss through a remarkable pre-Euclidean proof of :cite:`Elements I.5 <euclid:I.5>` utilizing mixed (rectilinear-curvilinear) angles of a circle!
